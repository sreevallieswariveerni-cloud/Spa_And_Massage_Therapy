Privacy Policy

How AuraSpa collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

At AuraSpa, we respect your privacy and aim to handle personal information responsibly. This policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit our website, contact us, create an account or request a wellness appointment.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, information may include:

  • Contact details such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Account information that you provide when registering.
  • Appointment requests, preferred treatments and booking details.
  • Messages, feedback or enquiries you send to us.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and website usage, where applicable.

Please avoid submitting sensitive medical information through a general website form.

3. How we use information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to enquiries and appointment requests.
  • Manage your account and communicate booking updates.
  • Improve website usability and our customer experience.
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.
  • Send promotional communications where permitted and, when required, with your consent.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website or embedded services may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functions, remember preferences and understand website performance. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

5. When information may be shared

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers who help operate our website, manage bookings or provide technical support, and where disclosure is required by law. Such sharing should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

6. Data retention and security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of online storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw consent for certain communications. You may also unsubscribe from promotional emails using the instructions provided in those messages.

8. Children's privacy

Our website is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the AuraSpa team through our Contact page.
